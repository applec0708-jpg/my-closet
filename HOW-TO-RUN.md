# Running the prototype

1. Unzip, then open `index.html` in Chrome or Safari. Double-clicking works.
2. Press **Start the prototype**, or **Start as a new user** for the setup flow.
3. Press **Reset prototype** on the index page to clear saved choices (avatar, uploads, saved looks).

**On your phone:** in this folder, run `python3 -m http.server 8000` on your laptop. Then open `http://<your laptop's IP>:8000` on your phone, on the same wifi.

**To record on a Mac:** open QuickTime Player, choose File, then New Screen Recording.
